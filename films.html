Architectural Film 2025

Spilaio

Set within the Pregame Concept Store and Bar, the film approaches architecture as a lived experience rather than a static object. The camera moves slowly through the space, paying attention to texture, proportion, and the quiet transitions between volumes. Arches, surfaces, and shadows are revealed gradually, allowing the atmosphere to unfold without interruption. The space is designed by Arpita Sharma, CEO of Atelier Astil, with a strong emphasis on materiality and spatial rhythm. My intention was to translate this language into film—maintaining restraint, continuity, and a sense of calm throughout the visual narrative. I was responsible for the film planning, direction, shot design, and editing, shaping Spilaio as a cohesive spatial journey. Each frame and cut is guided by the architecture itself, aiming to capture not only how the space looks, but how it feels when occupied. This film is an attempt to hold architecture in time—to let it breathe, pause, and speak quietly.

Architectural Film 2025

Casa Sereno

There is no rush in this film. Time stretches, light drifts, and spaces remain unspoken for just long enough. The camera listens more than it looks, allowing the house to reveal itself through silence, warmth, and restraint. The architecture is shaped by calm decisions—where materials breathe, daylight lingers, and interiors hold a sense of quiet assurance. The film listens closely to these moments, allowing silence, shadow, and proportion to guide the narrative. Here, luxury is not excess, but ease. Through careful pacing and restrained movement, the camera becomes a witness rather than a guide. It traces the subtle dialogue between inside and outside, between texture and touch, between structure and softness. I shaped the film through planning, direction, shot design, and editing, composing it as a slow spatial passage rather than a statement. Each frame is held long enough to be felt. Casa Sereno is not a portrait of a house, but of a state of mind—where architecture rests, and time slows.

Architectural Film 2025

CASA SERENO - NOCTURNE

As night settles in, artificial light reshapes the space—drawing out shadows, reflections, and a quieter intimacy. The architecture turns inward, guided by contrast, glow, and controlled darkness rather than openness. I led the direction, planning, cinematography, editing, and post-production, shaping the film as a slow, deliberate nocturnal study. In this state, the house feels less observed and more lived in—held gently by the night.

Diploma Film 2025

The Three Dot

The Three Dot is a short film developed as an exercise in restraint and visual clarity. Conceived and executed by me as writer, cinematographer, editor, and graphic designer, the project explores how narrative can emerge through pacing, framing, and visual rhythm rather than dialogue alone. The film reflects my interest in controlled storytelling and end-to-end authorship.
